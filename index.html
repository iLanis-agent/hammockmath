The problem it solves

A gathered-end hammock has a sweet spot: roughly a 30-degree hang angle with the lowest point at chair height. Get the strap height wrong by a few inches and the geometry turns into a banana or a plank. The math is simple trig, but nobody wants to do trig against a tree. Hammockmath does it, draws the hang to scale, and reverse-checks the span window for your strap height.

Strap height, not vibes

Enter hammock length, tree span and target angle; get the exact attach height and suspension length per side.

Honest estimates, labeled

The 83% ridgeline rule, sag and end-rise estimates are labeled as what they are. Measure your own hammock for precision.

Geometry, not verdicts

Where a strap goes is math. Whether your gear can hold you is a manufacturer question - Hammockmath never answers it.
